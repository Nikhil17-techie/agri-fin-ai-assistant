# AgriFin AI — Prompt Engineering Experiments

## Experiment 1: Agriculture Guidance

### Objective
Compare different prompting techniques for the same agricultural question.

### Test Question
My tomato plants have yellow leaves and slow growth.
What could be the reasons, and what should I do first?

### Technique 1: Zero-Shot Prompting

Prompt:
My tomato plants have yellow leaves and slow growth.
What could be the reasons, and what should I do first?

### Technique 2: Role-Based Prompting

Prompt:
Act as an agricultural guidance assistant.
My tomato plants have yellow leaves and slow growth.
What could be the reasons, and what should I do first?

Actual Output:
[Paste the response generated using your role-based prompt.]

### Technique 3: Structured Prompting

Prompt:
Act as an agricultural guidance assistant.
Explain possible reasons for yellow leaves and slow growth
in tomato plants. Provide the answer under these headings:
1. Possible Causes
2. First Steps
3. Precautions

Actual Output:
[Paste the response generated using your structured prompt.]

### Observation
The responses were similar in topic but differed in
how they presented the guidance.

### Conclusion
Prompt structure and instructions can influence the
organization and presentation of AI-generated responses.

## Experiment 2: Prompt Evaluation

### Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Does the answer address the user's question? |
| Clarity | Is the answer easy to understand? |
| Structure | Is the answer organized properly? |
| Actionability | Does the answer provide useful next steps? |
| Safety | Does the answer mention important precautions? |
